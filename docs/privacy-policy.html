Privacy Policy

Location Explorer — last updated August 8, 2026

Location Explorer ("the app") is a travel-information lookup app. This page explains what information the app handles and how, in plain terms.

Overview

The app is client-only — there is no backend server operated by the app that stores your data. Everything the app remembers (search history, cached location results, your settings) is stored locally on your device. The app does not require creating an account and does not include ads or analytics/tracking SDKs.

Information you provide

When you search for a place, the app sends your search text and/or the resulting coordinates to the third-party services listed below, in order to look up information about that place. The app does not collect your name, email, or any other personal identifier — it only knows the places you search for.

Device location

The app does not access your device's GPS/precise location. Every location lookup is based only on the place name you type into the search box.

Third-party services

Depending on what information you look up for a place, the app sends the place name and/or its coordinates directly from your device to one or more of the following services:

ServiceUsed forWhat's sent
OpenStreetMap (Nominatim / Overpass)Search, nearby places, airport, hotelsSearch text, coordinates
OpenWeatherMapCurrent weatherCoordinates
Open-Meteo7-day weather forecastCoordinates
TavilyWeb search grounding for AI-summarized content (optional)Generated search queries
Groq, or another AI provider you choose (Gemini / OpenRouter / OpenAI)Generating travel-info summariesPlace name, web search snippets
Firebase Remote ConfigOptional app configurationNo personal data — fetches static config values only

Each of these is a request straight from your device to that provider — the app itself never sees or stores this traffic anywhere else. Use of each service is subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

API keys

If you enter your own API key for a provider, it is stored only in your device's secure, encrypted storage (Android Keystore-backed) and is sent only to that provider when making a request on your behalf. It is never transmitted anywhere else.

Data stored on your device

  • Cached results for places you've looked up (so repeat lookups are fast and work offline for a while)
  • Your search history
  • Your app settings (language, units, etc.)

You can clear the cache and search history at any time from Settings → Data & Privacy. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
